Build requirements:
- A current TeX distribution containing elsarticle, amsmath, amsthm,
  mathtools, graphicx, booktabs, tabularx, microtype, siunitx, xcolor,
  hyperref, lineno, lmodern, and flushend.

From the source-directory root:

  latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error Manuscript_JCP.tex
  latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error Manuscript_JCP_clean.tex
  latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error Supplementary_Material_JCP.tex
  latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error Cover_Letter_JCP.tex

Expected outputs:
- Manuscript_JCP.pdf
- Manuscript_JCP_clean.pdf
- Supplementary_Material_JCP.pdf
- Cover_Letter_JCP.pdf

The authoritative bibliography is the numbered thebibliography environment in
references_jcp.tex, so no BibTeX pass is required. references.bib is supplied as
a machine-readable companion for the added root-isolation and software records.
Before journal upload, replace the Zenodo DOI placeholder and confirm the final
rendered manuscript, Supplementary Material, figures, and upload metadata.
