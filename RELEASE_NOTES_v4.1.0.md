# v4.1.0 — figure restyle and prose release

Supports *Complete-positivity regions of Runge–Kutta discretizations of phase-covariant qubit dynamics*,
by G. Blake Pierpoint, Olivier Bernard, and Yichen Liu.

The seven figures (three in the article, four in Online Resource 1) were restyled for the typeset article:
Computer Modern lettering, solid fills instead of hatching, consistent line weights, markers and panel
labels. The plotted data, series and panels are unchanged, and the figure contract tests were updated
for the new export settings. The article and Online Resource prose was edited; the flattened article source
shipped in `bit/manuscript_source/` was regenerated. No certified value, verification script or table
changed since v4.0.0.

The attached `BIT_Code_and_Data.zip` is the exact reviewed archive; the same 408 files appear under `bit/`,
byte-for-byte. The root historical tree and the v1.0.0, v4.0.0 and v4.0.1 tags and releases are unchanged.
Every release is archived on Zenodo under the concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.21522057](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21522057).
