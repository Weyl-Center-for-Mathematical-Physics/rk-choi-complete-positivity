# v4.1.2 — text revision of the article and supplement

Code and data for *Complete-positivity regions of Runge–Kutta discretizations of phase-covariant qubit
dynamics*, by G. Blake Pierpoint, Olivier Bernard, and Yichen Liu.

The article and Online Resource 1 were edited for readability, and the flattened article source in
`bit/manuscript_source/` was regenerated. No code, test, certified value, data record, figure or table changed
since v4.1.1.

The attached `BIT_Code_and_Data.zip` holds the same 408 files as `bit/` at this tag, byte for byte. Run from
either, `python reproduce_bit.py all` passes four stages and skips the audit of the article's LaTeX sources,
with 384 tests passed and 4 skipped. All versions are archived on Zenodo under the concept DOI
[10.5281/zenodo.21522057](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21522057).
